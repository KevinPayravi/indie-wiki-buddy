4.0 Update: Check your search engines

Sorry to interrupt! This is a one-time notice about an important change in Indie Wiki Buddy 4.0.

Indie Wiki Buddy used to have access to all supported search engines by default. Now it filters Google.com by default, and every other search engine is opt-in. Some of your old permissions may have carried over, so check the list below. Turn on any search engine you want, and uncheck any you don't want.

This change helps make Indie Wiki Buddy more privacy-friendly, and lets us more easily add new search engines in the future (which will also be opt-in).

🔍 

BreezeWiki permissions have changed too. If you were using a BreezeWiki mirror, you may need to grant permission for it again.

🍃
